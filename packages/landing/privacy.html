Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026 · covers yieldcircle.io, app.yieldcircle.io and the YieldCircle iPhone and Android apps

The short version. You need no account to use YieldCircle. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and we never sell data. Almost everything it shows is public blockchain data. What we hold beyond that: the email you give us for the waitlist, and whatever you choose to publish (a profile, comments, a linked X account, a paper account's name), which is public by design. We never hold your funds, keys or seed phrase.

Public blockchain data

Rates, markets, positions and the leaderboard are built from public on-chain data: wallet addresses, their transactions and their positions in lending markets. Anyone can read this from the blockchain. We index it to compute rates, positions and what a position made, and the board ranks public wallet addresses by it. Blockchain data cannot be changed or deleted by us or anyone else.

Your wallet

You connect a wallet you already use; YieldCircle never sees your keys or seed phrase, and every transaction is signed by you, in your wallet. On the phone, wallets connect through WalletConnect (Reown), whose relay passes messages between the app and your wallet. When you connect, the app reads your public address and balances to show your positions.

The waitlist and beta access

  • When you join the waitlist we store your wallet address, your email and the time, and the team gets a notification email with the same.
  • We use the email only to contact you about access to YieldCircle, including an invitation to test the iPhone app. If we invite you to the beta on iPhone, we give your email to Apple's TestFlight, which sends the invitation; Apple's own privacy policy then covers TestFlight.
  • Signing in to the web beta asks your wallet to sign a message; the app then keeps a cookie that says the wallet may enter. It holds nothing else.

What you choose to publish

Profiles, follows, reactions and comments are messages signed with your wallet and are public: anyone can read them, together with your address. You can delete what you posted. If you link an X account, the link between your X handle and your wallet is public too; we receive your handle from X through its sign-in and keep the short-lived sign-in state for ten minutes.

Paper money

A paper account is optional and opened with a name and a password. The password never leaves your device: together with the name it derives the account's key there. The name is public: it becomes the account's handle, on a profile marked as paper. Our server stores the paper account's practice trades under the address that key produces, and anyone can see them, as they can a wallet's.

On your device

Your settings (Lite or Pro, chains, filters) and caches are kept in your browser's storage or the app's storage on your phone. They do not leave your device. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it does.

Who we work with

  • Cloudflare hosts the websites and stores the waitlist.
  • WalletConnect / Reown connects your wallet to the app.
  • Resend delivers the team's waitlist notification emails.
  • Apple (TestFlight, the App Store) and Google (Google Play, Google Fonts) distribute the apps and fonts; TestFlight shares crash reports and the feedback you send us.
  • Blockchain nodes and 1delta's APIs answer the app's reads of public chain data and build the transactions you sign.

Keeping and deleting

We keep waitlist entries while the beta runs. Write to us to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it: we remove your waitlist entry, your email and your X link. On-chain data and what was signed and published may already have been copied by others and cannot be recalled. If you are in the EU or UK, you have these rights under the GDPR, and you may complain to your data protection authority.
